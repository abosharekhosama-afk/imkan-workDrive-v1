# Search and Discovery

## Global Search
- Searches across My Folders, Team Folders, and Shared with Me.
- Indexes file names, folder names, and file contents (text extraction - PROPOSED).

## Advanced Filters
- **File Type:** Document, Spreadsheet, Image, Video, Audio, Archive.
- **Location:** Specific Team Folder or My Folders.
- **Modified Date:** Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Custom Range.
- **Author/Owner:** Specific user.

## Empty States
- When no results found: "No matching files or folders found. Try adjusting your filters."
